Chapter I

The Method

We don't teach subjects.
We teach the student sitting in front of us.

Most academies run students through a syllabus. We run the syllabus through the student — adjusting pace, depth and drills to the one person doing the learning.

Diagnose

Find the exact gap.

Every student begins with a ninety-minute diagnostic. We don't guess at weaknesses — we map them, question by question, until we know precisely where understanding ends and memorization begins.

Focus

Six seats. No spectators.

Cohorts are capped at six. Every student is called on, every session, every week. Attention is the whole product — we refuse to dilute it.

Repeat

Practice with intent.

Spaced review is built into the calendar itself. What you learn on Monday returns on Thursday, then again in week three — until it's yours for good, not just for the test.
